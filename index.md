## Getting started

**What is Manna?**
Manna helps you build a steady routine with the daily text and daily Bible reading, and keep track of your field service hours. A pixel-art companion grows alongside your streak to keep you motivated.

**Which devices are supported?**
Manna runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later.

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can optionally sign in with Apple, but it isn't required. Your progress is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account.

**Can I choose what I see in the app?**
Yes. During setup you choose which parts you want to use: Daily text, Bible reading, Field service hours and News. You can switch them on or off later in Settings → Modules. Your data is kept when you switch something off.

## Bible reading

**How do reading plans work?**
Manna uses the official one-year Bible reading schedule. For the whole Bible you choose how long you want to take, from 1 to 7 years. Already reading the Bible? Pick the book and chapter where you are now, and the schedule continues from there.

**Can I read in chronological order?**
Yes. Besides the whole Bible, you can choose a chronological plan for the history of Israel or for the Christian congregation, or read by category. For these plans you choose how many days you spend on each portion.

**I missed a day. What happens to my schedule?**
Nothing is lost: the schedule waits for you. If you skip a day, the next day simply shows the portion you hadn't read yet, so there's nothing to catch up. Forgot to check off a day you did read? You can still check it off up to three days back via "Forgot to check off?".

**What if I read something else today?**
Tap "Read something else". Today then counts for your streak, and the planned portion stays ready for next time.

**Can I read more than one portion a day?**
Yes. Once today's portion is done, the Reading tab shows the next portion under "Read further".

**Can I change my reading plan later?**
Yes, you can adjust your reading plan at any time in Settings → Reading schedule: the type of schedule, your pace and your start date. Your progress is kept — Manna remembers which Bible passages you have read, so they still count after you change your plan. Only when you have finished a schedule completely and set a new reading goal does the new schedule start from the beginning.

**What happens when I finish my reading schedule?**
You get a celebration and can set a new reading goal right away. You can also choose "No goal for now": your streak is frozen and your companion goes to sleep until you set a new goal.

## Your streak and companion

**What is the companion for?**
When you start, you choose a dog, cat or sheep and give it a name. Your companion is linked to your daily streak: the longer your streak, the more it grows, through 16 stages with biblical outfits. You decide what counts for your streak: the daily text, Bible reading, or both.

**What happens if I miss days?**
Your companion gets hungry, then weakened and critical. After 14 missed days in a row it falls asleep in death. When you start again it comes back as a young animal (John 5:28, 29) and your streak starts over.

**Can I switch to a different animal?**
Yes. Go to Settings → Streak → Companion and choose a dog, cat or sheep. Your streak and stage stay the same — your new companion simply takes over where the old one was. You can also give it a new name right below.

**Can I use Manna without a streak?**
Yes. Switch off "Keep a streak" in Settings → Streak. You can still check off what you read, without a companion or streak counter.

## Field service hours

**How do I set a monthly goal?**
In Settings → Field service goals you can choose a preset goal, such as auxiliary pioneer (15 or 30 hours), regular pioneer (50 hours), circuit overseer (50 hours) or special pioneer (90 or 100 hours). You can also set a custom goal.

**Is there a yearly goal?**
Yes, but it's optional. It follows your monthly goal (12 times the monthly goal) and shows whether you are on schedule for the service year (1 September to 31 August). For auxiliary pioneers it is off by default; you can switch it on or off at any time.

**How are compensation hours for theocratic assignments handled?**
Manna follows the guidelines for pioneers:

- Compensation hours (for assignments and schools) are tracked separately and are never counted as field service hours.
- Field service and compensation hours combined count towards your goal up to a maximum of 55 hours per month, unless field service alone is higher.
- Hours above that limit are forfeited and do not carry over to the next month.
- Compensation hours should be reported in the remarks section of your report.

A new compensation entry defaults to 8 hours, but you can enter the actual time you spent.

**Is there a timer?**
Yes. Start the timer in the Service tab, from a widget or from Control Center. While it runs you see it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

**Does Manna submit my report for me?**
No. Manna only helps you keep track. You still submit your report yourself, as usual.

## Daily text and news

**Where can I find the daily text?**
Tap "Open daily text" and Manna opens the text for today on jw.org. Manna doesn't show the text itself.

**How do news articles open?**
On iPhone, articles open on jw.org in your default browser. On iPad, the article opens next to the list inside the app, with a button to open it in Safari.

**Can I get a notification when there's news?**
Yes. Switch on "New articles" in Settings → Notifications / Reminders. iOS decides when Manna checks in the background, so it can take a few hours.

## Appearance

**Can I change the colours?**
Yes. Manna offers four themes: Night Blue, Aubergine & Gold, Desert Sand and Olive & Honey. Each theme has a light and a dark version. In Settings → Appearance you choose light, dark or the same as your device.

## Sync and data

**Does Manna sync between devices?**
Yes. Your settings and progress are synced through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID on each device and that iCloud is turned on for Manna (Settings → [your name] → iCloud).

**My progress isn't showing up on my other device.**
Check that both devices use the same Apple ID, have an internet connection and have iCloud enabled for Manna. Syncing can sometimes take a few minutes.

**What happens if I delete the app?**
If iCloud sync is turned on, your data is restored when you reinstall Manna with the same Apple ID. Without iCloud, your data will be lost. You can also make a backup yourself in Settings → Export and import.

**Does Manna collect my data?**
No. Read the [privacy policy](https://github.com/Studio-NJV/Manna/blob/main/privacy.md) for full details.

## Troubleshooting

If something isn't working as expected:

1. Make sure you have the latest version of Manna from the App Store.
2. Close the app completely and open it again.
3. Restart your iPhone or iPad.

Still having trouble? Send an email describing the problem, along with your device model and iOS version. A screenshot helps a lot. The easiest way is Settings → Send feedback: your device details are filled in automatically.
