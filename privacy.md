# Privacy Policy for Manna

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Manna is an app for the daily text, daily Bible reading, field service hours and news from jw.org. It is made by Studio NJV. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use Manna.

**In short: Manna does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.**

## What Manna stores, and where

Manna stores the things you enter yourself, such as:

- which days you checked off the daily text and Bible reading, and which Bible passages you have read;
- your reading schedule, streak and companion (animal and name);
- your field service hours, theocratic assignments, compensation and goals;
- your settings, such as theme, language, modules and reminder times.

This information is stored **on your device**. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced through **your own private iCloud account** (Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage), so it is available on your other devices. Studio NJV cannot see or access this data; it is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Sign in with Apple

You can optionally sign in with Apple. Manna does not ask for your name or email address. It only receives an anonymous user identifier from Apple, which is stored on your device to check that you are signed in. It is not sent to us.

## Information we do not collect

Manna does not collect, sell or share personal data. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Connections to other services

- **News:** Manna downloads the public news feed from jw.org, together with the article images. These are ordinary requests to jw.org; no personal information from Manna is sent with them.
- **Articles:** on iPad (and other wide screens) an article opens inside Manna; on iPhone it opens in your browser. jw.org may then use cookies according to its own privacy policy, which you can accept or decline on the site.
- **Daily text and Bible passages:** buttons open the daily text on jw.org and Bible passages in JW Library or on wol.jw.org. What happens there is covered by the privacy policies of those services.
- Manna is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society or Jehovah's Witnesses.

## Notifications and widgets

Reminders and "new in News" notifications are created on your device. Widgets read your data on your device only.

## Export and feedback

- **Export:** when you export your data, Manna creates a file that you choose where to save or share. It is not sent to us.
- **Feedback:** if you tap "Send feedback", your mail app opens a message to us with the app version, build number, iOS version and device model filled in. You decide whether to send it. We only use your email to answer your feedback.

## Your choices

- You can delete individual entries in the app, or remove Manna to delete its data from your device.
- To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone or iPad.
- You can switch iCloud sync, notifications and every module on or off at any time.

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot view, change or delete it for you, but we are happy to help with any question.

## Children

Manna is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
